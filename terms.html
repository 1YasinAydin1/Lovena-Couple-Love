Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 17 October 2026

By using Lovena you agree to the following. We have kept it short.

Your account

You must be over 13 to create an account. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you tell us and for keeping your password safe.

Matching

Lovena is for two. An account can be matched with only one person at a time. Whoever you share your invite code with will see all of your shared content once you match.

Your content is yours

Everything you write, take and record belongs to you. We store it only in order to show it to you and your partner, and for no other purpose.

What is not allowed

Uploading someone else's content without permission, storing unlawful content, abusing the service, or accessing it with automated tools. We have zero tolerance for abuse: anything reported through Settings → Report abuse is reviewed within 24 hours, and accounts that break these rules can be closed.

Premium

Some features require a paid membership. Payment and cancellation run through the store you downloaded the app from, and refunds follow that store's rules.

The service

We are still building this, so features may change. We will try to announce planned downtime, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service.

Closing an account

You can end a connection or delete your account at any time. Ending a connection closes the shared space and keeps it for 30 days in case you reconnect. Deleting your account also deletes the space you shared; your partner keeps their own account. We may close an account for serious breaches of these terms.

Liability

Lovena is not a backup service. We recommend keeping your own copy of photos and recordings that matter to you.
