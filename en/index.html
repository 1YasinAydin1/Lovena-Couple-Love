Questions

Good to know

Is Lovena free?

Yes. Lovena is free to download and to use together every day. Plus and Gold are optional, and one subscription covers both of you.

What do Plus and Gold add?

More of everything you already love: more conversation packs and games, more flowers and vases for your garden, and more room to keep your photos, voice notes and memories for longer. Gold is the most generous.

Does my partner need the app too?

Yes, Lovena is made for two phones. One of you creates your space and sends an invite link or code; the other joins with it. iPhone and Android pair just fine.

What happens to our data if we delete our account?

Your account is deleted straight away. What you made together is kept for 30 days so the other person can download it, then it is erased for good, files included.

Which languages does Lovena speak?

Ten so far: Deutsch, English, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Türkçe, 한국어, 日本語, 繁體中文. Each of you can use the app in your own language.
